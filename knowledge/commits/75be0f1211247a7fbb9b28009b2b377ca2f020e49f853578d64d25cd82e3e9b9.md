# 2d0e0d9 移除 Indeed 與 XING 爬蟲，只保留 LinkedIn

## Summary
Removes the Indeed and XING scrapers and reduces the CLI to LinkedIn only. The platform selection prompt, per-platform search branches and detail dispatch in main.py are deleted, the banner and progress messages now say LinkedIn, and cloudscraper is dropped from requirements.

## Why
The commit message (in Chinese) states the intent directly: keep only LinkedIn. The other two scrapers depended on fragile anti-bot workarounds (cloudscraper for Indeed) and site-specific data formats, so narrowing scope simplifies maintenance.

## Risks
Any user relying on XING or Indeed DE results loses them. The job dict still carries a platform key defaulting to LinkedIn, so downstream display code keeps working.
