# f7af7de Add jobagent: agentic AI job search for Germany, Netherlands and Dublin

## Summary
Adds jobagent, an agentic pipeline for the newest AI engineering jobs in Germany, the Netherlands and Dublin: it collects from LinkedIn (3 regions x 8 role queries) and the public Greenhouse/Ashby/Lever boards of 51 AI companies, normalizes and dedupes across sources into SQLite, scores jobs against profile.md with OpenAI structured outputs, runs a bounded Responses-API research agent, and writes Markdown and Excel reports, all traced in Langfuse.

## Why
The user is job hunting for AI roles in DE/NL/Dublin and wanted an agentic search that covers company job boards LinkedIn misses, ranks postings against their profile and is observable in Langfuse.

## Risks
LinkedIn guest scraping at 24 searches per run risks rate limits (mitigated by pacing and a two-block circuit breaker). OpenAI cost scales with jobs scored (capped by JOBAGENT_MAX_SCORE_PER_RUN). Watchlist slugs can go stale; `companies verify` checks them. Tests that read the report without an explicit encoding fail on Windows (fixed in e842eb8).
