# 7206674 Add Personio, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Teamtailor and JSON-LD job sources (#4)

## Summary
Adds six job-board sources to jobagent (Personio XML, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, Workday's cxs API, Teamtailor RSS and schema.org JobPosting pages), a `companies detect <url>` command and agent tool that finds and live-verifies a company's board from its careers page, and grows the watchlist from 51 to 76 companies. German mandatory internships are now excluded.

## Why
Many AI employers in Germany and the Netherlands do not use Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever, so the watchlist and the research agent missed them.

## Risks
More live HTTP per run (Workday issues several keyword searches and detail lookups per company). JavaScript-only careers pages are still undetectable. Scraped HTML/JSON-LD formats can drift. PROMPT_VERSION bumped to 2026-09-27.1, so scores are not directly comparable with earlier runs.
